Layout tests

9 versions of the Gratovo homepage, each using a competitor's section order and effects with Gratovo's copy and colours. Every one speaks to both brands and creators. Open them one by one on desktop and phone. Sections that need proof Gratovo doesn't have yet (logos, testimonials, real creators) are left out or shown as clearly labelled examples.
