## Notes
- CC0 removes copyright limits, but the models' personality rights are not something CC0 covers. Keep the photos small, unnamed and clearly illustrative; never attach a name, channel or testimonial to one.
- Wikimedia Commons only (via curl with a descriptive User-Agent; Python's TLS store failed on this machine, so curl was used).
- A first search of Commons portrait categories (done by a subagent) found mostly historical or notable people and uniformed military photos, so it was dropped. The Unsplash/Pixabay CC0 copies above were the usable pool.
- Rejected on sight from the shortlist: full-body or distant shots, faces turned away, shirtless/tattoo shot, shots with people in the background (crowd could include women), and the older/heavily shadowed portraits.
