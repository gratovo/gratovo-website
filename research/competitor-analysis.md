## Patterns worth borrowing

- Stat/number strip straight after the hero (Attrakt, Apollo, Nsentive). We have no results yet, so Gratovo's strip shows the vetting standards instead.
- Process shown as a horizontal row of numbered steps (Apollo 5 steps, Attrakt 4, Nsentive 4). Gratovo uses 3.
- Pain-to-outcome headline with a single action (MindShare: "Stop guessing. Start converting." + "Book a Discovery Call").
- Three icon benefits in a row (Creator Authority).
- CTA visible in the hero, not only at the bottom.

## Weaknesses to avoid

- Vague hero lines that say nothing about value: Attrakt "Elevating influencer impact", Right Click "Let's make some cool sh*t".
- Copy that talks about the agency ("We are Attrakt", "WHO/WHAT/WHY" in Nsentive) instead of what the client gets.
- Proof sections (logos, testimonials, rosters) that Gratovo cannot match yet. Do not fake them.
