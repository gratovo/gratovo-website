Which is better?

  • No single winner to copy. Creator Authority and MindShare tie at 14. Both are short, but both are thin on telling the client what they get. Neither is a good template on its own.
  • Best headline and action: MindShare. "Stop guessing. Start converting." names a pain and an outcome, and the path ends in one action ("Book a Discovery Call"). Its weak spots are the vague hero tagline and the split between brands and creators.
  • Best process section: Apollo. Five numbered steps from proposal to analytics. Attrakt and Nsentive use four. Gratovo uses three, which suits a simpler offer.
  • Best number strip: Attrakt and Apollo. Big stats right after the hero. They rely on real results, so Gratovo shows its vetting standards instead.
  • Best brevity: Creator Authority. Seven sections, one clear audience for the brand. The copy stays generic ("Contact Us").
  • Worst fit: Right Click, then Attrakt. Right Click's hero says nothing about value and it targets entertainment. Attrakt is 12 sections of proof that a new agency cannot match.
  • Shared pattern: eight of the nine sites speak to both brands and creators. Gratovo does too, so copy that structure.
