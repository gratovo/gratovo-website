## Copy decisions (2026-10-08)

- Two tone versions to A/B test: `index.html` (bold, direct) and `index-b.html` (calm, professional). B is `noindex` and not in the sitemap.
- **What Gratovo actually does:** Deal Brokering and Roster Method / Campaign Sourcing. It puts 1-3 vetted creators forward, brokers and negotiates the deal, and handles contracts and logistics. It does **not** plan, strategise or run the brand's campaign, so avoid "plan a campaign", "campaign strategy" and "reporting and learning" language (that is how Scale Pledge-style agencies talk).
- Call to action: **"Find My Creators"**, a plain conversation starter. Tried and dropped, at the owner's request: a "free shortlist" lead magnet, a "pilot campaign", and "Plan My Campaign". None of the nine competitors uses a lead magnet or pilot; they all use plain actions ("Plan a Campaign", "Book a Discovery Call", "Let's Talk", "Contact"). See `competitor-copy.md`. What happens next is spelled out instead: tell us what you're promoting, we reply within 24 hours with the creators we'd pick (Nsentive promises a 24-hour reply too).
- **Both sides:** every page speaks to brands AND creators (two hero buttons "Find My Creators" / "Get Brand Deals", a For Creators section, a form for each). Creator pitch (owner-chosen): "Brand deals come to you" and "One contact, less back-and-forth". Payment is deliberately not mentioned.
- Voice is "we". Brand name is "Gratovo". No margin or commission talk. No proof claims.

## Page claims the owner has NOT confirmed (check before launch)

- **"We reply within 24 hours."** A promise only the owner can keep.
- **No pricing, rates, fees, or payment handling is mentioned anywhere on the pages** (owner's instruction). Do not reintroduce "flat price", "fair rates", "negotiate", "cost", or similar.
- The visitor **approves the video before it goes live**.
- Campaigns go live **within about a month** (course: 14-30 days).
- **Reporting and scaling** (owner asked for it): the pages say we track how each video performs and help the brand scale with the creators who deliver (and line up repeat deals for creators). Only the owner can confirm Gratovo reports results and handles repeat deals.
- **Creator-side steps** (channel review, brand matching, offers, brand approval, repeat deals) are the owner-approved structure but the exact promises are unconfirmed.
- The "8 found, 3 vetted" matching animation is illustrative, not a statistic.
- Vetting strip shows the course's standards: 30K+ average views, 3-8% engagement, 35%+ audience in the target country, 1-3 creators. Change them if Gratovo's real standards differ.
- A view guarantee is in the course but is deliberately **not** on the site.

## Three pages (2026-10-08)

`index.html` (both audiences), `brands.html` and `creators.html` are **generated** by `scripts/build-pages.py` from `pages/partials/*.html` plus the hero, process-step, FAQ and creator-benefit data inside the script. Edit those, then run `python research/scripts/build-pages.py`; hand edits to the three pages are overwritten. The hero is the MindShare-style top section (giant wordmark, bracketed tagline, pill buttons, platform ticker); home buttons are "I'm a Brand" / "I'm a Creator" (to the two pages), sub-page buttons are "Find My Creators" / "Get Brand Deals". On brands/creators pages the How It Works and FAQ sections show only that side (no tabs, they ignore the contact tabs), while the contact section always keeps both tabs and starts on the page's side. `index-b.html` (calm A/B copy) is **not** generated and is now out of date with these pages.

### Creator photos in the matching card
The brand-side "Creator Matching" and "Reporting & Scaling" cards can show real creator photos. Put permitted photos at `images/creators/creator-1.jpg` ... `creator-8.jpg` (jpg, png or webp, roughly square, face near the top) and rerun `build-pages.py`; only files that exist are used and any missing slot keeps the neutral avatar. Use only photos of creators who agreed to appear, or that you took/own. Stock or public-domain portraits of strangers would present them as Gratovo creators, so they were deliberately not used.

### Hero colours
The hero is white text on the brand-blue gradient (the earlier near-black on blue was about 3.6:1 contrast, below the 4.5:1 minimum for small text). Nav has Home, For brands, For creators, Contact; Home shows as a house icon on phones.

### Font flicker fix (2026-10-08)
- Cause: all faces used `font-display: swap`, only 2 of 5 were preloaded, icons came from Google (icon names showed as text) and the Tailwind CDN builds its CSS after first paint.
- Fix: `research/pages/partials/font-gate.html` hides `body` until the fonts are loaded and Tailwind has applied (2.5 s cap, then it shows anyway). All five faces are preloaded. Material Symbols is now a self-hosted subset (`fonts/material-symbols-subset.woff2`, ~15 KB, `font-display: block`).
- Icons in the subset: arrow_forward, bolt, chat, check, check_circle, cloud, code, content_copy, database, description, event_available, groups, handshake, home, hub, mail, memory, person, rocket_launch, schedule, search, send, smart_display, smart_toy, terminal, trending_up, upload, verified, visibility.
- **If you add a new icon**, re-fetch the subset: request `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Material+Symbols+Outlined:wght,FILL@100..700,0..1&icon_names=<sorted,comma,list>&display=block` with a Chrome user agent, download the woff2 URL it returns to `fonts/material-symbols-subset.woff2`. An icon missing from the subset shows as its name in text.

### Static Tailwind build (replaces the CDN and the font gate) — 2026-10-08
- The Tailwind CDN script built its CSS in the browser after first paint, which caused the reload flash. I first masked it with a "gate" (hide the page until ready), but that itself flashed bright blue and then white. The gate is removed.
- Now the pages link `css/tailwind.css` (23 KB, compiled, render-blocking, so the first paint is already styled). `fonts.css` uses `font-display: block` and all five font files are preloaded, so there is no fallback-font flash either.
- Workflow after changing copy/classes/colours: `python research/scripts/build-pages.py` then `python research/scripts/build-css.py` (needs Node; first run installs tailwindcss 3.4.17 + forms + container-queries into `research/.tools/`, git-ignored). Theme colours still live in `research/pages/partials/tailwind-config.html`. If you forget to rebuild the CSS after adding a new Tailwind class, that class will have no styling.
- Sticky nav: `site_header()` in the generator; it is a direct child of `<body>` (so it also stays over the footer). The Contact button is the phone emoji (aria-label "Contact us").
- Not migrated: `privacy-policy.html`, `terms-of-service.html`, `index-b.html` still use the Tailwind CDN.
